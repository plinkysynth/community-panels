# SPIEGEL 12

[Download the PDF manual](manual.pdf)

An interpretation of Laurie Spiegel's Music Mouse for Plinky 12. Your finger is the mouse: it steers four interlocking voices, all snapped to a harmonic mode, so every move sounds musical.

**See it in action:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/Demx5sICVLa/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&srtk=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==


## Play

- **Right field (12 x 16):** touch to play. Columns drive the X voice pair, rows drive the Y voice pair. Higher row = higher pitch.
- **Voice markers:** blue/purple on the bottom edge (X pair), green/red on the right edge (Y pair).
- **Quantised:** the touched cell is adopted on the next 1/16 grid line (switch to Off for instant response).
- **Free-running clock:** no transport needed.

## Left strip

**Pressure pads** (top, hold and press harder for more):

| Pad | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Vibrato | Pitch wobble |
| Tremolo | Level wobble |
| Filter | Closes the low-pass |
| Accent | Harder hits |
| Delay | Delay send (shares a slot with Filter, the harder press wins) |
| Random | Moves played notes a few scale steps; never triggers on its own |

**Tap pads** (below the pressure pads):

- **Mode:** Minor pentatonic, Harmonic minor, Major 9, Diatonic, Pentatonic, Quartal.
- **Motion:** Parallel or Contrary.
- **Treatment:** Chord, Arpeggio, Line, Improv.
- **Articulation:** Staccato, Half legato, Legato. **Latch** holds the cursor after you lift off.
- **Patterns:** 10 note patterns (first pad = off).
- **Quantise:** Off or 1/16.
- **Interval / Transpose:** minus and plus pads.
- **X / Y octave:** minus and plus pads per voice pair.
- **Sound 1-3:** three sounds. **Edit sound** opens the sound editor.

## Pages

- **Edit sound:** synth preset editor with XY pad. Use the bottom-left pads to switch between Edit and Load.
- **Save / Load:** scroll right from the play page to store and recall panel states.
