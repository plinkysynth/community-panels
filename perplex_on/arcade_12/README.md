# ARCADE12

See it in action: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfKoD-MMp0r/?srtk=MXhuZHg5YWU1MmFuZA==

Two games that write their own soundtrack, one cartridge slot on Plinky 12. Pick a game, play, and the music follows every move.

- **Selector:** Tap the left demo for KLOTZ, the right one for PRALL.
- **Pad > in a game:** tap = play / pause, hold 0.7 s = back to the selector. A run in progress waits there, paused.
- **KLOTZ** (falling blocks): swipe ← → moves the stone, swipe ↓ drops it on the next 16th, tap rotates. The stack is a 10-step sequencer against the 16-step bar; landing strums a chord, cleared lines move the harmony, four lines at once modulate up a fourth. One more layer per level.
- **PRALL** (breakout on the clock): drag moves the paddle, tap or swipe ↑ launches. Brick row = height in the chord, brick column = which chord tone. Cleared rows move the chords, every level is a new song. Power-ups: HALF · DOUBLE · MULTI · WIDE.
- **Sound:** each game keeps its own voices (KLOTZ 9, PRALL 7), mix and MIDI channels.
- **High scores:** top 5 per game, kept across reboots. After game over the score scrolls across the grid; a new best plays a fanfare.
- **Pages:** Play / Sound (current game, bottom row selects + auditions) / Save-Load (both sound sets).
- **Settings:** root, scale and MIDI per game, KLOTZ also the chord progression — saved globally.
