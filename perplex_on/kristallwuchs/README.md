# KRISTALLWUCHS

[Download the PDF manual](manual.pdf)

Crystal growth is an algorithmic instrument for Plinky 12. Random walkers freeze onto a growing crystal and every docking plays a note.

See it in action: https://www.instagram.com/reel/Ddo3G4rCksZ/?stkn=ajlnaTB4YjJzdjIw

- **Seeds:** tap an empty pad to plant a seed (max 4), tap a seed to remove it with its branch. No seeds = silence.
- **Notes:** distance to the seed sets the scale degree (core low, tips high), each seed has its own octave. Always clock-quantized.
- **Melt:** at the edge the crystal melts from the outside in, plays its growth backwards, then regrows.
- **Touch:** hold = walker emitter (pressure = rate), swipe = wind, tilt the device = gravity.
- **Overlay:** long-press the bottom-right pad for density, walk speed, stickiness, tilt amount, panic and reset seeds.
- **Settings:** root, 8 scales, quantize division (1/4 to 1/32, incl. triplets).
- **Pages:** Play / Sound / Save-Load.
