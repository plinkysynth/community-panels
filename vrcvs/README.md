# VRCVS

I play drums in Fine Before You Came, and I make music
as VRCVS and lua: it started from dark, new wave
and post punk roots and keeps drifting towards ambient,
generative music and soundscapes.

## Panels

- **[QU4TTRO](qu4ttro/)** — four melodic tracks side by side, plonky-style,
  each with its own looper, length and direction. Polyrhythmic loops on the
  Chords faceplate.
- **[N3VE](n3ve/)** — generative ambient, ported from my norns script NEVE:
  snow, tears and a live voice that answers your phrases. On the Chords
  faceplate.
- **[DER1VA](der1va/)** — a polymetric 4-track sequencer freely inspired by
  Kria: every parameter has its own loop and speed, with harmony, drift and
  chance. On the Chords faceplate.
