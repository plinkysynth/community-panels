# N3VE

Generative ambient instrument for the Chords faceplate, ported from the norns script NEVE. Snow falls in the sky and plays low notes when it lands, the tears answer with a melody, and the third voice is yours, on a keyboard that always fits the chord.

- **Chords on the top row**: tap a degree, the chord changes at the end of the bar; or let the progression run.
- **Tap the sky** to drop flakes; **play a phrase** on the live keyboard and the tears answer it (call and response, on by default).
- **3 voices** with their own sound, octave, mute and MIDI channel; 12 scales and any key; three styles for the tears.
- **MIX / SND / PRE**: mixer, sound editing, system picker and your own banks on the SD card.
- **Song save/load** with the right side buttons.

[PDF manual](manual.pdf) (EN). Italian manual and changelog: https://github.com/vrcvs/plinky12_n3ve

**Your own multisample banks:** the Plinky has 14 MB of sample memory. To use 3 of your banks at once, build them in the [Bank Builder](https://vrcvs.github.io/plinky12_bank_builder/) with *Banks loaded together: 3* (about 4 MB each).
