# DER1VA

Polymetric 4-track sequencer for the Chords faceplate, freely inspired by Kria (monome). Every parameter (rhythm, notes, octaves, lengths, repeats) has its own loop and speed, so the tracks drift against each other and the music never repeats the same way.

- **4 tracks**, each MONO, CHRD (ready-made chords from key and scale) or POLY, on Kria-style pages: rhythm, NOTE, OCT, LEN, REP, each with its own LOOP and SPD.
- **Harmony**: key, 12 scales and a progression of up to 8 chords; tracks set to FOL follow it, the others play freely in scale.
- **Drift and chance**: melodies that slowly wander in scale, per-step variations of ±1–3, and new sequences generated in key.
- **6 patterns**, each a full scene, with copy, clear and chain; mutes, FILL, SKIP and a keyboard in scale for playing live.
- **PRE / MIX / SND**: system picker with your own banks on the SD card, a 4-track mixer, and Plinky's full sound and effects editor.

Start with "Try it in two minutes" on page 1 of the [PDF manual](manual.pdf) (EN). Italian manual and changelog: https://github.com/vrcvs/plinky12_der1va

**Your own multisample banks:** the Plinky has 14 MB of sample memory. To use 4 of your banks at once, build them in the [Bank Builder](https://vrcvs.github.io/plinky12_bank_builder/) with *Banks loaded together: 4*.

Freely inspired by [Kria](https://monome.org) by monome; no Kria code is used. By VRCVS – Instagram [@vrcvs](https://www.instagram.com/vrcvs/). MIT licence.
